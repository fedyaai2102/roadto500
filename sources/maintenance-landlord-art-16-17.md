# Landlord's maintenance duty (and handover in good condition)
- **Figure / rule:** Unless agreed otherwise, the landlord is responsible during the term for maintenance and for repairing defects/damage affecting the tenant's intended use; and for wear and tear not caused by the tenant's fault. Property must be handed over in good condition.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 16 and Art. 17 (not amended); Art. 15 as superseded by Law 33/2008
- **Quote:** Art. 16: "Unless otherwise agreed by the parties, the Landlord will, during the term of the Lease Contract, be responsible for the Real Property' maintenance works and for repairing any defect or damage that may affect the Tenant's intended use of the Real Property." Art. 17: "the Landlord will be responsible for any defect, damage, deficiency, and wear and tear occurring to the Real Property for reasons not attributable to the fault of the Tenant." Art. 15 (amended): "A Landlord will be bound to hand over the Real Property in a good condition which allows the Tenant to fully use it as stated in the Lease Contract."
- **Source:** Arts 16–17: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2007/Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007%20Regarding%20regulation%20between%20the%20lessor%20and%20the%20lessee.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict). Art. 15: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html (official; same).
