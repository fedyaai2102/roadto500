# Eviction before expiry (during the term), incl. non-payment: 30 days after notice
- **Figure / rule:** Landlord may seek eviction before expiry only on the listed grounds; for non-payment, the tenant must have failed to pay within 30 days of service of a Notice to pay; notice must be served via Notary Public or registered mail.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 25(1)(a)–(i) as superseded by Law 33/2008
- **Quote:** "(a) unless otherwise agreed by the parties, where the Tenant fails to pay the Rent or any part thereof within thirty (30) days from the date of service of a Notice on the Tenant by the Landlord requesting the payment;" … "For the purposes of paragraph (1) of this Article, the Landlord will serve a Notice on the Tenant through a Notary Public or by registered mail."

- **Notes:** Expected values confirmed (Art. 25(1), 30 days). Other grounds (summary): (b) unapproved subletting; (c) illegal/immoral use; (d) business premises left unoccupied without valid reason 30 consecutive / 90 non-consecutive days a year (unless agreed otherwise); (e) dangerous changes or wilful/grossly negligent damage; (f) use for another purpose or breach of planning rules; (g) property likely to collapse (Dubai Municipality technical report); (h) breach of any obligation not cured within 30 days of a Notice; (i) government-mandated demolition/reconstruction. The 30-day period in (a) can be changed by contract ("unless otherwise agreed").
