# What counts as a "Notice" (general service methods)
- **Figure / rule:** A Notice is a written notification sent through a Notary Public, or delivered by registered mail, by hand, or by other technological means approved by law. Eviction notices under Art. 25 are stricter: Notary Public or registered mail only.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 2 (definition "Notice") as superseded by Law 33/2008; Art. 25 as superseded
- **Quote:** "A written notification sent by either party to a Lease Contract to the other party through a Notary Public; or delivered by registered mail, by hand, or by any other technological means approved by law."
- **Source:** https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** For a 90-day amend-terms / rent-increase notice (Art. 14) the general definition applies (hand delivery or approved electronic means are allowed by the text). For eviction (Art. 25(1) and 25(2)) use Notary Public or registered mail only. The RDC eviction filing requires "A copy of Notorised Notice along with notification officer's report or Registered Post along with receipt of Notice acknowledgement in case of eviction claim" (https://rdc.gov.ae/en/eservices/register-first-instance-lawsuit-rental/, official). Which electronic means are "approved by law" is not defined in the law — VERIFY before recommending email/WhatsApp service.
