# Re-letting bar after eviction for own use: 2 years residential / 3 years non-residential
- **Figure / rule:** If the RDC grants repossession for own/first-degree-relative use, the landlord may not lease to a third party for at least 2 years (residential) or 3 years (non-residential) from repossession; otherwise the tenant may claim compensation.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 26 as superseded by Law 33/2008
- **Quote:** "the Landlord may not rent the Real Property to a third party before the lapse of at least two (2) years in case of residential Real Property, or three (3) years in case of non-residential Real Property, from the date of retaking possession of the same."
- **Source:** https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** Original 2007 text said one calendar year — superseded.
