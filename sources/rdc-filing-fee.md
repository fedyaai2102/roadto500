# RDC filing fee (first-instance rental case)
- **Figure / rule:** 3.5% of annual rent (eviction, renewal, rent claim, termination, return after demolition): min AED 500, max AED 20,000. Monetary claims: 3.5% of the claimed amount, min AED 500, max AED 15,000. Plus process service AED 100, knowledge fee AED 10, innovation fee AED 10, power of attorney AED 25 if applicable; trustee-centre filing adds AED 130 + VAT.
- **Law / article:** Decree 26/2013 Art. 23 (fees by Executive Council resolution); RDC service page "Register First Instance Lawsuit (Rental)"
- **Quote:** "3.5% of Annual Rent or Lease Value if it is more or less than a year in the following cases (eviction, lease renewal, rent claim, termination of lease in force, and return to the premises previously evicted from for demolition and reconstruction - Fee not less than (AED 500) and not more than (AED 20,000)" … "3.5% of Claimed Amount in case of monetary Claims - Fee not less than (AED 500) and not more than (AED 15,000)" … "Process Service: AED 100" "AED 10 Knowledge fee" "AED 10 Innovation fee"
- **Source:** https://rdc.gov.ae/en/eservices/register-first-instance-lawsuit-rental/ (official RDC service page, English original). Fee power: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2013/Decree%2026%20of%202013.html Art. 23 (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict).
