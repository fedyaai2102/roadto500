# Tenancy disputes body: Rental Disputes Center (Decree No. 26 of 2013)
- **Figure / rule:** The Rent Disputes Settlement Centre (now branded "Rental Disputes Center", at DLD) has exclusive jurisdiction over Dubai landlord–tenant rent disputes, including free zones, with listed exceptions. It replaced the Special Tribunal under Decree 2/1993.
- **Law / article:** Decree No. (26) of 2013 Art. 4(b), Art. 5, Art. 6
- **Quote:** Art. 6(a)(1): "The Centre will have the exclusive jurisdiction to: 1. determine all Rent Disputes that arise between Landlords and Tenants of Real Property situated in the Emirate, including in free zones, and counterclaims arising therefrom, as well as determine applications for interim or urgent relief filed by any of the parties to a Lease Contract;" Art. 4(b): "The name "Special Tribunal to Determine Disputes between Landlords and Tenants" is hereby replaced by the name "Rent Disputes Settlement Centre in the Emirate of Dubai"."
- **Source:** https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2013/Decree%2026%20of%202013.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict). RDC official site: https://rdc.gov.ae/en/about-rdc/about-rental-disputes-center/ ("The Rental Disputes Center in Dubai is an integrated judicial system ...").
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** Issued 18 September 2013; in force 60 days after publication (Art. 31). Head office in DLD (Art. 5). Excluded (Art. 6(b)): free zones with their own rent tribunals/courts; lease-finance contracts; long-term leases under Law 7/2006. Structure includes a Mediation and Conciliation Directorate; Art. 10(i): "One-half of the fee will be refunded if amicable settlement is reached between the parties to the Rent Dispute". Fees are set by Executive Council resolution (Art. 23) — current amounts in rdc-filing-fee.md.
