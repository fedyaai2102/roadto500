# Dubai tenancy law: Law 26/2007 as amended by Law 33/2008
- **Figure / rule:** Law No. (26) of 2007 regulates landlord–tenant relationships in Dubai; Law No. (33) of 2008 supersedes 11 of its articles (2, 3, 4, 9, 13, 14, 15, 25, 26, 29, 36). Always read the two together.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 1 (title), Art. 37 (commencement); Law 33/2008 Art. 1 and Art. 2
- **Quote:** Law 26/2007 Art. 1: "This Law will be cited as "Law No. (26) of 2007 Regulating the Relationship between Landlords and Tenants in the Emirate of Dubai"." Law 33/2008 Art. 1: "Articles (2), (3), (4), (9), (13), (14), (15), (25), (26), (29), and (36) of the Original Law are hereby superseded by the following"
