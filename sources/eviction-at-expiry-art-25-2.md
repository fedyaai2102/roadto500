# Eviction at expiry (sale, own use, demolition, renovation): 12 months' notice
- **Figure / rule:** At expiry, eviction only for: demolition/reconstruction or new structures (with permits); restoration/comprehensive maintenance impossible with tenant in place (Dubai Municipality report); owner/first-degree relative own use (owner must prove no suitable alternative property); sale. Notice of reasons at least 12 months before the eviction date, served via Notary Public or registered mail.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 25(2)(a)–(d) as superseded by Law 33/2008
- **Quote:** "For the purposes of paragraph (2) of this Article, the Landlord must notify the Tenant of the eviction reasons at least twelve (12) months before the date of eviction, and the notice must be served through a Notary Public or by registered mail."

- **Notes:** Expected values confirmed (12 months; notary or registered mail). Note the wording: 12 months before "the date of eviction" (Arabic: the date set for eviction), not literally "before expiry" — the calculator should count back from the intended eviction date, which can be no earlier than contract expiry. The ORIGINAL 2007 text said 90 days; it was superseded — never use 90 days for these grounds. Own-use condition: "provided that the owner proves that he does not own any alternative Real Property that is suitable for this purpose". Sale ground text: "(d) the leased Real Property owner wishes to sell it." Re-letting bar after own use: see own-use-relet-bar-art-26.md.
