# Tenant's duty: pay on time and maintain the property as an ordinary person would
- **Figure / rule:** Tenant must pay rent on due dates and maintain the property as an ordinary person maintains his own; no changes, restoration or maintenance works without landlord permission and required licences (except agreed or customary tenant restorations).
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 19 (not amended)
- **Quote:** "The Tenant must pay the Rent on due dates and maintain the Real Property in such a manner as an ordinary person would maintain his own property."
- **Source:** https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2007/Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007%20Regarding%20regulation%20between%20the%20lessor%20and%20the%20lessee.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** Continuation: "the Tenant may not make any changes or carry out any restoration or maintenance works to the Real Property unless so permitted by the Landlord and after obtaining required licences from the competent official entities." Art. 23: unless agreed otherwise, tenant may not remove leasehold improvements on vacating.
