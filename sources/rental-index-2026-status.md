# DLD rental index: Smart Rental Index (status as of Nov 2026)
- **Figure / rule:** DLD launched the "Smart Rental Index 2025" on 2 January 2025 (AI-based, with building classification). It supplies the "average rent" used in the Decree 43/2013 slabs. The DLD "Rental Index" e-service calculates the allowed increase from contract expiry date, property type, area, rooms and current annual rent.
- **Law / article:** Decree 43/2013 Art. 3 (average from RERA Rent Index); DLD announcements (not legislation)
- **Quote:** DLD, 2 Jan 2025: "Dubai Land Department launches the Smart Rental Index 2025" … "This index relies on artificial intelligence technologies and a building classification system". DLD, 16 Feb 2025: "the applicable increase based on data drawn from multiple factors, including rental contract values in the building, the average rental values in the area, and the building classification." DLD e-service: "This service allows a customer to calculate the rental increase and the average rental in the real estate market by entering data for the required area."

- **Notes:** No official DLD statement dated 2026 found today that changes the index or the slabs; the DLD site footer shows "last updated 29 October 2026". Secondary sources (blogs, Aug 2026) say it is still in use — not relied on. The index covers residential areas; commercial/industrial indexes were "plans" in Jan 2025 — VERIFY before using for commercial units. CAUTION: the 2 Jan 2025 DLD release contains two errors — it cites "Decree No. (34) of 2013" (the decree is No. 43) and says increases "reach up to 20% for rents that exceed the average by more than 40%" (the decree says rent "more than forty percent (40%) less than" the average). Cite the decree, not the press release, for the slabs. The calculator's internal method (rounding, building star rating weights) is not published — VERIFY; tell users to run the official calculator.
