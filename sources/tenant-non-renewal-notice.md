# Non-renewal notice (tenant or landlord) — no statutory period in amended law
- **Figure / rule:** The amended law sets no general notice period for simply not renewing. If the tenant stays on without objection, the lease renews for the same term or one year (whichever is shorter) on the same terms. A landlord can only refuse renewal/evict at expiry on Art. 25(2) grounds (12 months' notice).
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 6 (not amended); Art. 14 and Art. 25(2) as superseded by Law 33/2008
- **Quote:** Art. 6: "Where the term of a Lease Contract expires, but the Tenant continues to occupy the Real Property without any objection by the Landlord, the Lease Contract will be renewed for the same term or for a term of one year, whichever is shorter, and under the same terms as the previous Lease Contract." Art. 25(2) (amended): "Upon expiry of the Lease Contract, the Landlord may seek eviction of the Tenant from the Real Property only if:"
- **Source:** Art. 6: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2007/Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007%20Regarding%20regulation%20between%20the%20lessor%20and%20the%20lessee.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict). Arts 14 and 25: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html (official; same).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** VERIFY. Original 2007 Art. 14 covered a party who does "not wish to renew" (90 days); the amended Art. 14 (English and Arabic) covers only amending terms. A tenant's non-renewal notice period therefore depends on the contract (many contracts specify 90 days — check the customer's own contract; do not interpret it for them). No official DLD/RDC page found today stating a statutory tenant non-renewal period. Templates should say "[notice period under your tenancy contract]" rather than a fixed figure.
