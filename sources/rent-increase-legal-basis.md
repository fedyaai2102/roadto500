# Legal basis for rent-increase caps (which article links increases to RERA criteria)
- **Figure / rule:** Rent can be reviewed only at renewal (Art. 13); RERA sets the criteria for rent-increase percentages (Art. 10); the Tribunal applies those criteria (Art. 9(2)). The actual caps are in Decree 43/2013.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 10 (not amended); Art. 9 and Art. 13 as superseded by Law 33/2008
- **Quote:** Art. 10: "RERA will have the authority to establish criteria relating to percentages of Rent increase in the Emirate in line with the requirements of the prevailing economic situation in the Emirate." Art. 9(2) (amended): "The Tribunal will determine the rental value of similar Real Property taking into account the criteria for determining the percentage of Rent increase prescribed by RERA"
- **Source:** Art. 10: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2007/Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007%20Regarding%20regulation%20between%20the%20lessor%20and%20the%20lessee.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict). Arts 9 and 13: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html (official; same).

- **Notes:** DIFFERS FROM EXPECTED: amended Art. 9 does not itself tie increases to a decree or index — it covers specifying the rent and how the Tribunal sets the rental value of similar property. The power to set increase percentages is Art. 10. The original 2007 Art. 9 contained a two-year freeze on rent increases ("may not be increased ... before the lapse of two years"); Law 33/2008 superseded Art. 9 and the new text has NO two-year freeze.
