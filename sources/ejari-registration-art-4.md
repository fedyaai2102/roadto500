# Registration of tenancy contracts with RERA (Ejari)
- **Figure / rule:** All lease contracts under the law, and their amendments, must be registered with RERA (done through DLD's Ejari system). Online registration fee: AED 177.75 total; at a Real Estate Services Trustee Centre: AED 220 total.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 4(2) as superseded by Law 33/2008; DLD e-service "Register / Renew Ejari Contract"
- **Quote:** Art. 4(2): "All Lease Contracts related to Real Property which is governed by the provisions of this Law, and any amendments thereto, will be registered with RERA." DLD fees: "1)AED 100 for registering a tenancy contract 2) AED 10 knowledge fee 3) AED 10 innovation fee 4) In addition, the service partners' fees of AED 55 plus a value-added tax (VAT) on the service partner fee of AED 2.75 Total: 177.75 AED" (via Dubai REST app / DLD website); trustee centres: partner fee "AED 95 + VAT" … "Total: 220 AED".
- **Source:** https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2009/Law%20No.%20(33)%20of%202008%20Amending%20Law%20No.%20(26)%20of%202007.html (official; official English translation by the Supreme Legislation Committee, Dubai; the page states the Arabic text prevails in case of conflict). Fees: https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/register-renew-ejari-contract (official DLD service page, English original).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** Expected Art. 4 confirmed. The word "Ejari" does not appear in the law; Ejari is DLD's system name (DLD page: "This service allows customers to register or renew a Tenancy contract in the Emirate of Dubai"). The original 2007 Art. 4(2) added that authorities "may not consider any dispute ... unless such Contract is registered with RERA"; that sentence is NOT in the amended 2008 text. In practice the RDC requires "A copy of latest lease (Ejari)" to file a case (https://rdc.gov.ae/en/eservices/register-first-instance-lawsuit-rental/, official). The law does not say whether landlord or tenant pays — VERIFY. Fees can change; recheck monthly.
