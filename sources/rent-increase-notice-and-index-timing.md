# Rent increase needs both 90-day notice AND index eligibility (DLD mechanism)
- **Figure / rule:** An increase applies only if the landlord gave notice at least 90 days before expiry AND the Smart Rent Index confirms eligibility; without the 90-day notice, no increase even if the index allows one.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 14 as superseded by Law 33/2008; Decree 43/2013; DLD implementation statement of 16 Feb 2025
- **Quote:** "if a landlord notifies the tenant of a rent increase at least 90 days before the contract's expiration, the increase will be applied only if the Smart Rent Index confirms the property's eligibility." … "but the landlord did not notify the tenant at least 90 days before the contract's expiration. In that case, the increase will not be applied."
- **Source:** https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/the-smart-rent-index-mitigates-inflation-in-dubai-and-enhances-market-transparency/ (official DLD press release, English original)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-03
- **Notes:** The same release has a 2025 transition rule (old index for renewals before 2025, new index for renewals during 2025) — now historical. It is a press release, not legislation; the binding rule is Art. 14 (see notice-90-days-amend-terms.md).
