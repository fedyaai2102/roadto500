# Notice to amend tenancy terms (incl. rent) at renewal: 90 days
- **Figure / rule:** A party wanting to amend any term of the lease (including the rent) at renewal must notify the other party at least 90 days before the contract expires, unless the parties agreed otherwise.
- **Law / article:** Law 26/2007 Art. 14 as superseded by Law 33/2008 (read with Art. 13 as superseded)
- **Quote:** "Unless otherwise agreed by the parties to a Lease Contract, where either party wishes to amend any of its terms pursuant to Article (13) of this Law, that party must notify the other party of this intent no less than ninety (90) days before the date on which the Lease Contract expires."

- **Notes:** Expected value confirmed (Art. 14, 90 days). SURPRISE: the amended Art. 14 covers only AMENDING terms. The original 2007 Art. 14 also covered a party that does "not wish to renew"; that wording was removed in 2008 (see tenant-non-renewal-notice.md). "Unless otherwise agreed" means the contract can set a different period. Art. 13 (amended): parties may "prior to the expiry of the Lease Contract, amend any of its terms or reconsider increasing or reducing the Rent"; if no agreement, "the Tribunal may determine the fair Rent". DLD's own statement that an increase is not applied without 90 days' notice: see rent-increase-notice-and-index-timing.md.
