# Sources index — Dubai tenancy templates

All figures were retrieved and checked on **2026-11-03** from the pages linked in each file. Primary texts: Dubai Legislation Portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae; the English is the official Supreme Legislation Committee translation, and the Arabic text prevails), Dubai Land Department (dubailand.gov.ae), Rental Disputes Center (rdc.gov.ae), Government of Dubai Media Office (mediaoffice.ae).

Key point: the English DLP page for Law 26/2007 shows the **original 2007 text**. Articles 2, 3, 4, 9, 13, 14, 15, 25, 26, 29 and 36 were replaced by Law 33/2008. Always cite the 2008 wording for those articles.

Re-verify every file each month and bump its Retrieved date.

General information and templates only, not legal advice. For your specific case consult a licensed legal practitioner in the UAE.

## Open VERIFY items
1. Non-renewal notice period: none in the amended law. Templates should use "[notice period under your contract]".
2. Decree 43 bands at fractional gaps (e.g. 10.5%) and the DLD calculator's rounding and building-rating method are not published.
3. Which "technological means approved by law" count as valid Notice (email, WhatsApp).
4. Cost of a notification officer serving a notarised notice; private notary office fees.
5. RDC amicable-settlement filing fee (the page refused the connection today) and the number of the Executive Council fee resolution.
6. Who pays the Ejari fee (the law is silent).
7. Smart Rental Index coverage of commercial and industrial property.
8. Shared-housing Law 4/2026 and federal Decree-Law 25/2025 (Civil Transactions): seen in secondary sources only, not used.
