# Who may provide "Legal Services" to the public in Dubai (licensing risk)
- **Figure / rule:** In Dubai, "Legal Services" are provided to the public by Advocacy Firms and Legal Consultancy Firms licensed by the Legal Affairs Department (LAD). The definition of Legal Services includes "providing legal opinion and advice; drafting contracts; ... and providing other legal services".
- **Law / article:** Dubai Administrative Resolution No. (52) of 2022 Approving the Bylaws Concerning the Licensing (Art. 2 definitions; Art. 3 scope); read with Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2022 (legal profession) and Dubai Law No. 32 of 2008 (LAD licensing power)
- **Quote:** "Legal Services: The legal services which Legal Consultancy Firms are licensed to provide in the Emirate. These include, but are not limited to, providing legal opinion and advice; drafting contracts; registering and liquidating companies; concluding settlements; representing others before arbitration tribunals and centres, conciliation committees, and other entities; and providing other legal services ..." Art. 3(b): "The provisions of this Bylaw do not apply to Government Entities, to corporations and companies that do not provide Legal Services to the public ..."
- **Source:** https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2022/Administrative%20Resolution%20No.%20(52)%20of%202022%20Approving%20the%20Bylaws%20Concerning%20the%20Licensing.html (official; official English translation, Arabic prevails). Opened and searched in full by Claude on 2026-11-03.

- **Notes:** OPEN QUESTION, not a conclusion. The bylaw defines what licensed firms do; it does not, in the passages read, say in so many words that an unlicensed person may not fill a tenancy notice for a customer. Federal Decree-Law 34/2022 Art. 5 ("Drafting the contracts and relevant legal procedures") is known only from search extracts (official site returned 403) — VERIFY. Whether filling a tenancy notice/letter template with a customer's facts is "drafting contracts" or "relevant legal procedures" is for a licensed UAE lawyer or the LAD to answer. Selling blank templates that the buyer fills in themselves is a different activity from per-customer drafting — ask about both.
