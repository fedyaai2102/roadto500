## 1. Fiverr rules that apply

**Prohibited: legal services without authorisation**
- Fiverr's prohibited-services list includes "providing legal services without the required authorization". It says Fiverr "does not allow users to offer professional services without the credentials required to provide them lawfully or responsibly". It also bans dressing such services up "under misleading titles, descriptions, or categories". (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/49174165608593-Prohibited-services-on-Fiverr, retrieved 2026-11-03
- The Community Standards list "licensed professional services without the required certifications and/or qualifications, ex. legal counsel, financial or tax advice" as prohibited. (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37554466620305-Community-Standards-Illegal-and-prohibited-services, retrieved 2026-11-03
- Regulated professions: "certain regulated professions (like lawyers, accountants…) require specific licenses and certifications". If you add one to your profile, you must hold those qualifications. (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37332925319185-Creating-a-profession-in-accordance-with-Fiverr-s-policies, retrieved 2026-11-03
- **Do non-lawyers need credentials?** Only for *legal services or advice*. None of the snippets requires credentials for writing documents or templates, and none forbids it. Fiverr does not screen legal-service providers. It tells buyers to check them themselves (TBIJ report, Nov 2025). (search snippet) https://www.thebureauinvestigates.com/stories/2025-11-26/scammers-use-ai-to-impersonate-real-lawyers-on-fiverr, retrieved 2026-11-03. I found no 2026 change to how Fiverr checks legal sellers: VERIFY.

**Misrepresentation and misleading gigs**
- You may not "pose as another person or entity in a misleading manner". You may not "write misleading content in your profile description, profession profile, skills section, professional background, and/or education". Where a service needs credentials, sellers must represent their qualifications accurately. (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/32243087473553-Integrity-and-Authenticity, retrieved 2026-11-03
- One gig violation is "Misleading/Unclear Descriptions": a "Gig description, title or packages were found to be unclear or misleading". Copied descriptions and images are also violations. (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37555045126289-Gig-violations, retrieved 2026-11-03
- **No Fiverr page found that names specific words** such as "lawyer", "attorney", "legal advice", "guaranteed", "official" or "government-approved". The words to avoid in section 5 follow from the rules above (no claiming credentials, no misleading claims). They are not a published Fiverr word list. VERIFY on the Gig violations page.

**Contact details and payment outside Fiverr**
- Do not "propose or agree to bypass Fiverr's payment system". Do not share "email addresses, phone numbers, or third-party messaging apps with the sole purpose of conducting business off-platform". Off-platform contact can lead to account suspension. (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/38728907371665-Community-Standards-Off-platform-policy, retrieved 2026-11-03

**UAE law (outside Fiverr, but relevant)**
- Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2022 regulates the advocacy and legal consultancy professions and came into force on 2 January 2023. Secondary summaries say it "prohibits unlicensed practice". (search snippet) https://nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/a3002b1d/new-uae-and-dubai-laws-bring-in-tighter-regulation-of-uae-legal-professionals and https://kbh.ae/the-regulation-of-advocacy-and-legal-professionals-in-the-uae, retrieved 2026-11-03

## 4. Disclosing AI-assisted deliverables

- Sellers are "not required to disclose their tools in Gig descriptions". They "must disclose their use of AI tools when asked by clients". They must "honor a client's explicit request for non-AI work". (search snippet) https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593-Using-AI-on-Fiverr-Guidelines-for-freelancers-and-clients, retrieved 2026-11-03
- AI-assisted work must be "high-quality, original, and meaningfully refined and customized to the client's specific requirements". Generic or reused AI output does not meet the standard. Sellers must not deliver the same AI content in bulk to many clients. (search snippet) same page and https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333179414289-Community-Standards-AI-generated-content, retrieved 2026-11-03
- **For this gig:** every order must be filled with the buyer's own facts, never a bare master template. Answer honestly if a buyer asks about AI. Turn down orders from buyers who ask for no AI if the workflow uses it.

## 5. Verdict: **YES, WITH CONDITIONS**

The gig is document drafting from templates. It is not legal advice or representation, and it does not depend on holding a credential. That fits the rules found, as long as nothing in the gig claims or suggests legal services. Every rule above came from search results, so Fedya should confirm the linked Fiverr pages once before he publishes.

**Conditions**
1. List the gig under a legal *writing / documents* subcategory, not "Legal Consulting" (check names live: VERIFY).
2. Profile and gig say what he is: "12 years in Dubai real estate", not a lawyer. Do not add a regulated "Lawyer" or "Legal consultant" profession. Do not name his employer.
3. Every delivery and the gig description carry the disclaimer: "General information and templates only, not legal advice. For your specific case consult a licensed legal practitioner in the UAE."
4. Scope line in the gig and FAQ: "I do not review or interpret your contract, advise on your case, or represent you." Refuse orders that ask for this, and offer a cancellation.
5. Each deliverable is customised to the buyer's form. No identical AI output to many buyers. Disclose AI use if asked.
6. All contact and payment stay inside Fiverr: no phone, WhatsApp, email or website link in the gig, profile or messages. Product links in the gig description: VERIFY that they are allowed before adding the store link.
7. Cite the law as "based on Law No. 26 of 2007 (as amended)". Never suggest that RERA, DLD or any authority endorses the documents.
8. Complete ID verification, and check which payout methods the UAE account shows. Keep records for tax. Settle the UAE licence question (section 2) before income becomes regular.

**Words and phrases to avoid** (based on the credential and misleading-content rules, not a published Fiverr list)
- Credentials: "lawyer", "attorney", "advocate", "legal consultant", "solicitor", "paralegal", "law firm", "counsel".
- Advice: "legal advice", "legal opinion", "consultation", "I will review your contract", "advise you on your case", "your rights in your case", "win your dispute", "represent you", "court-ready".
- Outcomes: "guaranteed", "100% valid", "legally binding guaranteed", "enforceable", "get your deposit back guaranteed".
- Authority: "official", "government-approved", "RERA-approved", "DLD-approved", "certified", "Ejari-approved", logos of RERA, DLD or Dubai government.
- Contact: "WhatsApp me", phone numbers, email addresses, "pay me directly", outside payment links.

**Safe phrasing:** "Dubai tenancy document templates filled with your details", "notice and letter drafting", "plain-English glossary", "based on Dubai tenancy law (Law 26/2007 as amended)", "templates, not legal advice".
