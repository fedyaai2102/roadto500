- All credentials are the sellers' own claims. None were checked against a UAE licence register.

## (d) Gaps our gig could fill

1. **Nobody found sells Dubai landlord/tenant letters as a product.** The Dubai gigs are Ejari admin (#1, #2), off-plan sale-contract review (#5) and RDC dispute consultation (#3, #4). No snippet showed a gig for a Dubai rent-increase notice, a non-renewal notice, a deposit deduction letter or a deposit-return demand. A title naming those documents with "Dubai tenancy" in it would stand almost alone in search.
2. **A move-out / deposit bundle.** No competitor offers a checklist plus deduction letter plus deposit-return letter as one package. Our Premium tier ($120) has no direct match.
3. **A plain-English RERA clause glossary** in the Standard tier: not seen anywhere.
4. **Honest "documents, not advice" positioning with cited sources.** Most competitors lean on "lawyer" or "legal consultant" claims, several of them from outside the UAE. A gig that says clearly it delivers templates filled with your facts, with the governing rule and an as-of date cited on every document, is a distinct and safer offer. It also avoids the advice and credential claims that the rules research (`/fiverr/research.md`) needs to clear.
5. **The low-review field favours a new entrant.** The only Dubai-specific gig with a visible count has 1 review (#3). A zero-review start is less of a handicap here than in the generic eviction-notice niche, where gigs have 25–130+ reviews. Still, the three friends-and-family orders in Step 8 matter.
