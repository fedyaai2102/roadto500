## 1. Does the gig fit Fiverr's rules?

**YES on the Fiverr rules found; UAE-law risk OPEN.**

- **Fiverr:** bans legal *services/advice* without credentials. Drafting documents from templates with the buyer's own facts is not banned by any rule found, as long as nothing claims or implies legal advice or credentials.
- **UAE law (applies to every channel, including WhatsApp and brokers):** Federal Decree-Law 34/2022 regulates the legal profession. A search result for its official page lists "Drafting the contracts and relevant legal procedures" within the licensed scope. Custom-drafted notices may fall inside it. The **dispute summary** (move-out pack) is the highest-risk item. Official article text not yet opened: **VERIFY**.

Conditions (full list in `research-rules.md` §5):
1. List under a legal writing / documents subcategory, not "Legal Consulting" (exact name: VERIFY).
2. Profile says "12 years in Dubai real estate". Never "lawyer", "legal consultant", or a regulated profession. No employer name.
3. Disclaimer in the gig and on every delivery.
4. Scope line: no contract review, no advice on the buyer's case, no representation. Refuse such orders.
5. Every delivery customised to the buyer's form; disclose AI use if asked.
6. No phone, WhatsApp, email or outside payment anywhere on Fiverr. Store link in the gig: VERIFY.
7. Never suggest RERA, DLD or any authority approved the documents.
8. Finish ID verification; check payout methods shown to a UAE account.
9. **Before the first paid order on any channel:** short check with a licensed UAE legal practitioner or Dubai DET on Decree-Law 34/2022 and the dispute summary.

**Words to avoid** (our list, not a Fiverr-published list): lawyer, attorney, advocate, legal consultant, legal advice, legal opinion, consultation, review your contract, guaranteed, 100% valid, official, government/RERA/DLD/Ejari-approved, certified, WhatsApp me (on Fiverr).
