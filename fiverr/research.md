## 1. Does the gig fit Fiverr's rules?

**YES, with conditions.** Fiverr bans legal *services/advice* without credentials. Drafting documents from templates with the buyer's own facts is not banned, as long as the gig never claims or implies legal advice or credentials.

Conditions (full list in `research-rules.md` §5):
1. List under a legal writing / documents subcategory, not "Legal Consulting" (exact name: VERIFY in the live dropdown).
2. Profile says "12 years in Dubai real estate". Never "lawyer", "legal consultant", or a regulated profession. No employer name.
3. Disclaimer in the gig and on every delivery.
4. Scope line: no contract review, no advice on the buyer's case, no representation. Refuse such orders.
5. Every delivery customised to the buyer's form; disclose AI use if asked.
6. No phone, WhatsApp, email or outside payment anywhere on Fiverr. Whether a store link is allowed in the gig: VERIFY.
7. Never suggest RERA, DLD or any authority approved the documents.
8. Finish ID verification; check payout methods shown to a UAE account.

**Words to avoid:** lawyer, attorney, advocate, legal consultant, legal advice, legal opinion, consultation, review your contract, guaranteed, 100% valid, official, government/RERA/DLD/Ejari-approved, certified, WhatsApp me.

**Outside Fiverr (Fedya's own risk, not a Fiverr rule):** UAE sources say a trade licence is needed to run any business; Federal Decree-Law 34/2022 bans unlicensed legal consultancy. Both: VERIFY. This is why the E-Trader licence question should be settled before income is regular.
