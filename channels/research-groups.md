# Where Dubai landlords and owners ask questions online: Telegram and Facebook

Research date: 2026-11-03. Every link below was retrieved 2026-11-03.

General information only, not legal advice.

## How this was researched (read first)

- **Method:** WebSearch only. WebFetch to `freten.ru` and `tgstat.ru` was blocked by this session's network policy. Following that guidance, I did **not** fetch any `t.me` or Facebook pages directly. To fetch them, the hosts must be added under *Allowed domains* in the cloud environment's Network access settings.
- **What "(snippet)" means:** the figure comes from a search engine's cached copy of the public page. The date of that copy is **unknown**, so every count is "as last indexed, seen 2026-11-03". Treat each one as approximate and **VERIFY in the app**.
- **Telegram group or broadcast channel:** a t.me page that says "members" belongs to a **group**, where members can post. One that says "subscribers" belongs to a **channel**, where only admins post. Channels are flagged **BROADCAST**.
- **Rules:** most rules below are search-summary paraphrases of the group description, not the full pinned rules. Exact wording is marked VERIFY.
- **Main finding:** I found **no** Telegram or Facebook group that is specifically "Dubai landlords asking questions" and large.
  - Russian speakers ask landlord and tenant questions in (a) big rental boards and (b) big general "life in Dubai" chats.
  - English-speaking owners ask mostly in big Facebook expat groups and a few property groups. English Telegram is dominated by brokers and listings.

## 5. Shortlist: join these 4 first

1. **RU: Дубай ЧАТ** (https://t.me/dubai_chat_russia). The largest Russian Dubai discussion group found (47,737 members, live count 2026-11-03). Its stated purpose is "communication and help", so it is the best place for helpful answers on rent, Ejari and deposits. No promotion without admin approval.
2. **RU: Чат взаимопомощи** (https://t.me/russkie_dubai). Small (4,049 members, live count 2026-11-03), but its description says service offers are allowed. It is the one Russian place where mentioning the templates may be OK (VERIFY).
3. **EN: Dubai Expat Community** (Facebook). The largest English Dubai Q&A group (about 94K–150K members, sources disagree). Tenancy questions come up there, and it reaches both tenants and owners.
4. **EN: Dubai Real Estate Market: Buy, Sell, Rent** (https://www.facebook.com/groups/dubairealestatemarketbuysellrent/). The only English group found where an owner was seen asking peers about managing tenants. The audience is closest to landlords.

Runners-up:
- https://t.me/rent_in_dubai: the biggest Russian rental board (about 70K members), but posts go through a bot and links are banned.
- The Dubai Investors Group: exact audience, but closed and small.

## 6. What Fedya must check by hand after joining

1. **Current member count and activity.** Note the count, messages per day and the date. Every count here is a cached snippet of unknown age.
2. **Pinned rules and the group description in full.** Copy the exact text into this file (wording here is paraphrased). For Facebook, check the "Rules" tab.
3. **Group or broadcast channel.** On Telegram, can members post or only comment? Check whether a linked discussion chat exists (chatrudubai, Dubilook).
4. **Self-promotion policy.** Are links, service offers or a profile mention allowed? Is there a promo day, a paid ad slot, or a bot or admin approval step? For example, `Dubai_chat` lists a USD 200 unban fee. Never post a link until this is confirmed.
5. **Posting mechanics.** Does a post go through a bot (rent_in_dubai, Русские в Дубае), is there a mandatory format or hashtag, and is there a language rule?
6. **Content mix.** Scroll the last 2 weeks. What share is real owner or tenant questions versus agent listings and spam? Drop groups that are mostly listings.
7. **Typical questions.** Log recurring questions (deposits, rent increase, notice, Ejari, bounced cheque) to feed the articles and the calculator. Patterns only, never anyone's personal details.
8. **Admin contact.** For any group where promotion is unclear, ask an admin before posting a link, and keep the reply.
9. **Disclaimer fit.** Make sure an answer can carry the "general information, not legal advice" line. Never interpret a member's own contract in public.
10. **Separation.** Use a personal or new-brand profile only. Never mention the employer, buildings or tenants, and never mention the other project (DLandKit).
11. **The overseas-owner gap.** Search Facebook in the app for "Dubai landlords", "Dubai property owners", "UK owners Dubai property" and "NRI Dubai property". Add any group with more than 5K members to this file.
