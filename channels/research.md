## 0. Decide this first — legal-services risk ⚠️

| What | Status |
|---|---|
| Dubai Administrative Resolution 52/2022 lists "drafting contracts" among the Legal Services that LAD-licensed firms provide to the public | **Confirmed from official text** (opened 2026-11-03) |
| Federal Decree-Law 34/2022 Art. 5 lists "Drafting the contracts and relevant legal procedures" as legal-profession activity | Search extract only (official site returned 403): VERIFY |
| DET lists "Legal Consultancy" as an activity that needs Legal Affairs Department approval | Secondary source: VERIFY |
| Ziina lists "legal service providers" as a restricted business | Provider page via search extract: VERIFY |

**What this means in plain words:** filling a notice for a paying customer may count as a legal service that only licensed firms can offer in Dubai. Selling blank templates that buyers fill in themselves is further from that line. Neither is settled. An E-Trader licence does not fix this if the activity itself needs LAD approval.

**Three questions for a licensed UAE lawyer** (full wording in the licence file §5.1):
1. Is filling tenancy notice/letter templates with a customer's facts "drafting contracts / relevant legal procedures" under Decree-Law 34/2022 and AR 52/2022? Does the answer change for (a) per-customer filling, (b) blank self-fill templates, (c) a branded template pack licensed to a brokerage?
2. Is the "dispute summary" in the move-out pack a legal service?
3. Which licence or activity code covers what is allowed, and what is the penalty if this is misjudged?

## 4. Selling to a brokerage

- VAT registration only above AED 375,000 of taxable supplies a year (official FTA). Below that, a plain invoice, no VAT.
- Corporate tax for an individual's business only above AED 1,000,000 turnover a year (Cabinet Decision 49/2023, official).
- National e-invoicing for businesses under AED 50M starts 1 July 2027; whether it covers a small non-VAT seller: VERIFY.
- Beyond the licence question in §0, nothing extra found for B2B at this size.
