### 1.2 Who is eligible: this is the main uncertainty

- **Older official-era reports say it is for nationals only.** A 2017-era report says "the owner must be an Emirati or GCC citizen aged 18 or above and residing in Dubai." [SECONDARY] Khaleej Times, https://www.khaleejtimes.com/life-and-living/visa-and-immigration-in-uae/dubais-e-trader-explained-licence-for-freelancers-at-their-residence-offers-three-visas, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **The same outlet reports that expats can hold it for some professional activities.** A DET call-centre agent told Khaleej Times that an e-Trader licence "for commercial activities (such as trading) will only be issued to UAE and GCC nationals." The licence "can be issued to expats for some professional activities (some activities in the service sector)." [SECONDARY] Khaleej Times, https://www.khaleejtimes.com/life-and-living/dubai-trade-licence, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **2026 consultancy pages disagree with each other.** Some say expat residents with a valid residence visa and Emirates ID can apply for professional (service) activities. Others say non-GCC expats are not eligible at all. One claims DET opened an "Online Seller" activity (code 6312009) to all nationalities, which no official source confirms. [SECONDARY] https://bestaxca.com/e-trader-license-dubai/, https://avyanco.com/e-trader-license-dubai/, https://tmgdubai.ae/blogs/ded-trader-license-dubai-2026/, https://dubaieconomycenter.ae/knowledge-center/e-trader-license, all retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Employer NOC: VERIFY.** Consultancy guides say an expat on an employment visa "may need" a No Objection Certificate from the sponsor. No official source confirms it. [SECONDARY] https://tlz.ae/e-trader-license-in-dubai/ and https://arnifi.com/blog/how-to-get-e-trader-licence-dubai, retrieved 2026-11-03. Your written employer approval may serve as this NOC, but ask DET what format it needs.

**Bottom line on eligibility:** an expat resident appears able to get only a professional (services) e-Trader licence, if any. Confirm this with DET before you pay anything. Call 600 545555 or use the Invest in Dubai portal. (VERIFY: the call-centre number is not from a retrieved source.)

### 1.3 What it allows, and its limits

- **Purpose:** to license "freelancers at their place of residence in Dubai" and businesses "online or on social networking sites." [OFFICIAL] Dubai Media Office, https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2022/Jan/24-01/e-Trader-licence-records, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **No shop:** "The licensee cannot open a shop/store." The licence carries visas only if it is 100% Emirati-owned. [OFFICIAL] same Dubai Media Office page, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Services are allowed, not only goods:** consultancy pages say both goods and services are covered, and that expats are limited to services. [SECONDARY] Virtuzone and Shuraa, https://www.shuraa.com/dubai-e-trader-license, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Selling on social media or WhatsApp:** the scheme was built for this. [SECONDARY] Gulf News, https://gulfnews.com/technology/ded-launches-licences-for-doing-business-on-social-media-1.1994570, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Regulated activities are excluded until approved:** licensees "must refrain from selling any goods or services that require special approval from the competent authority, until such approvals are obtained." [SECONDARY, DET wording quoted] https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/e-trader-license-dubai, retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Document preparation, typing and consultancy: VERIFY.** No source names "typing" or "document preparation" as an allowed e-Trader activity. Two things are reported:
  - The DET activity list shows "Legal Consultancy" (code 6910010) as "requires approval from Legal Affairs Department". [SECONDARY] Virtuzone DED activity list, https://virtuzone.com/blog/ded-activity-list/, retrieved 2026-11-03.
  - One guide says real-estate brokerage is not allowed on the e-Trader licence. [SECONDARY] https://egsh.ae/services/e-trader-licence-dubai, retrieved 2026-11-03.
  
  So legal consultancy cannot be the activity. Ask DET for the exact activity name and code that fits "preparing standard documents from templates", for example a typing service or document-preparation activity. Ask whether it needs any outside approval.

## 3. Selling to a brokerage (B2B)

- **VAT:**
  - Registration is mandatory above **AED 375,000** of taxable supplies in the past 12 months, or expected in the next 30 days.
  - Voluntary registration is possible from **AED 187,500**.
  - [OFFICIAL] FTA, https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/vat.topics/registration.for.vat.aspx ; Cabinet Decision 52 of 2017, https://tax.gov.ae/-/media/Files/FTA/links/Legislation/VAT/03-Cabinet-Decision-52-of-2017.pdf , retrieved 2026-11-03.
  - At your price points you are far below the mandatory threshold.
- **Tax invoices:**
  - Only a VAT "Registrant" making a taxable supply must issue a Tax Invoice (VAT Decree-Law 8 of 2017, Art. 65). [OFFICIAL] https://tax.gov.ae/-/media/Files/FTA/links/Legislation/VAT/01-VAT-Decree-Law-No-8-of-2017.pdf , retrieved 2026-11-03.
  - Before you register, issue a plain invoice with no VAT and without the words "Tax Invoice". (VERIFY the exact FTA wording for unregistered sellers.)
  - A brokerage cannot reclaim VAT on your invoice. Some may still ask for your trade licence copy as a supplier.
- **E-invoicing, new and worth watching:**
  - The national e-invoicing system covers B2B and B2G transactions of "any person conducting business in the UAE", subject to the exclusions in Art. 4 of Ministerial Decision 243 of 2025.
  - Businesses with revenue under AED 50 million must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by **31 March 2027** and go live by **1 July 2027**.
  - The penalty is AED 5,000 per month of delay.
  - [OFFICIAL] MoF, https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Ministerial-Decision-no.-243-of-2025-on-the-Electronic-Invoicing-System.pdf ; MoF news, https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-announces-targeted-amendments-to-einvoicing-system-decisions/ ; FTA, https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/federal.tax.authority.calls.on.persons.subject.to.einvoicing.to.comply.wit.the.prescribed.legal.deadlines.and.avoid.administrative.penalties.aspx , retrieved 2026-11-03.
  - **VERIFY:** whether a small natural person who is not VAT-registered and sells to brokerages is in scope. Selling only to consumers appears to be out of scope for now. [SECONDARY] Crowe, https://www.crowe.com/ae/news/uae-introduces-nationwide-electronic-invoicing-system , retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Corporate tax for a natural person:**
  - Business income of a natural person is subject to Corporate Tax "only where the total Turnover derived from such Businesses or Business Activities exceeds AED 1,000,000" in a calendar year (Cabinet Decision 49 of 2023, Art. 2(1)). [OFFICIAL] https://tax.gov.ae/DataFolder/Files/Legislation/05-06-2023/3/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2049%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf , retrieved 2026-11-03.
  - At or below AED 1 million, "they do not have to register for, or pay, Corporate Tax." Your salary is excluded from the turnover count.
  - Above AED 1 million, you must register by 31 March of the following year. The late-registration penalty is AED 10,000.
  - [OFFICIAL] FTA, https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/corporate.tax/corporate.tax.topics/basis.of.taxation.natural.person.aspx ; https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/CT/Taxation%20of%20natural%20persons%20-%2025%2011%202023.pdf , retrieved 2026-11-03.
  - A licence alone does not trigger registration; turnover does (same sources).
- **Beyond E-Trader for B2B:** no extra registration found below these thresholds. The open question is the legal-services risk in section 4, which applies more strongly when you are paid by a regulated real-estate company.

## 4. Legal-profession risk

### 4.1 Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2022

The law's official English title is "Regulating the Legal Profession and Legal Consultation Profession". It came into force on 2 January 2023. Its executive regulations are Cabinet Resolution No. 8 of 2025.

Official text (not opened, see the note at the top): [OFFICIAL] https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1566 and the MoJ copy, https://www.moj.gov.ae/assets/d984aaa6/federal-decree-law-no-34-of-2022-regulating-the-legal-profession-and-legal-consultation-638441976591014059.aspx , retrieved 2026-11-03. The quotes below are the search engine's extracts of that official text.

- **Article 5, activities of the legal profession:** "The legal profession's activities shall include, without limitation, the following work:
  - a. Appearing on behalf of, and representing, the persons concerned before the courts, the public prosecution, the committees of a judicial mandate, the investigation bodies and the police …
  - b. Giving legal opinion and advice;
  - c. Drafting the contracts and relevant legal procedures; …"
  
  A further item covers representing parties in non-judicial legal work.
  
  The article number comes from the official text per the search extract. Two secondary sources (Mondaq, Legal-Wires) cite it as Art. 6, so VERIFY the number against the gazette.
- **Definition of the legal consultation profession:** "giving the legal opinion, advice and consultation, drafting and drawing up the contracts and documents and relevant legal procedures, and other profession-related services that do not require appearance with the litigants or representing them." (official text per search extract; article number VERIFY)
- **Penalties, secondary and unconfirmed:**
  - A summary lists imprisonment of up to one year and/or a fine of up to AED 100,000 for "practicing the legal profession without being listed in the Roll" (Art. 97), and the same for using the title "lawyer" (Art. 98).
  - A separate extract of the official text gives Art. 98 as at least three months and/or AED 30,000–100,000 for a different act.
  - The numbering and amounts conflict. VERIFY. [SECONDARY] https://legal-wires.com/uae/federal-decree-law-regulating-the-legal-profession-and-legal-consultation-profession ; https://kbh.ae/the-regulation-of-advocacy-and-legal-professionals-in-the-uae , retrieved 2026-11-03.

### 4.2 Dubai's own rules

- **Licensing authority:** Law No. 32 of 2008 gives the Government of Dubai Legal Affairs Department (LAD) power to license "the advocacy and legal consultancy profession" and firms. [OFFICIAL] https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2008/Law%2032%20of%202008.pdf , retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Administrative Resolution No. 52 of 2022 (licensing bylaw):**
  - "Legal Services may only be provided to the public through a Legal Consultancy Firm licensed by the LAD."
  - "Legal Services" for consultancy firms "include, but are not limited to, providing legal opinion and advice; drafting contracts …"
  - The bylaw does not apply to "companies that do not provide Legal Services to the public."
  - [OFFICIAL] https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2022/Administrative%20Resolution%20No.%20(52)%20of%202022%20Approving%20the%20Bylaws%20Concerning%20the%20Licensing.html , retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Other Dubai rules:** Administrative Resolutions 51, 54 and 55 of 2022 cover visiting consultants, discipline and violations. Executive Council Resolution 22 of 2011 sets fees and fines; the fine amounts were not found (VERIFY). [OFFICIAL] https://legal.dubai.gov.ae/en/services/pages/advocates-and-legal-consultants-bylaws-and-resolutions.aspx , retrieved 2026-11-03.
- **Enforcement:** an older press report says no one may provide legal services, "including … drafting contracts", unless licensed by the LAD. [SECONDARY] The National, https://thenationalnews.com/business/dubai-lawyers-to-require-licences-from-next-month-1.435230 , retrieved 2026-11-03.

### 4.3 Where the line seems to be (not a legal conclusion)

**The texts restrict:**

- giving legal opinion or advice
- drafting contracts, documents and "relevant legal procedures"

**Your products:**

- **Inside the plain words?** A tenancy notice or deposit-demand letter filled with a customer's facts is a "document" with a legal effect. The rent-increase or non-renewal notice is the step that starts a legal procedure. Filling one for a named customer is close to "drawing up … documents and relevant legal procedures". Choosing which notice applies, which notice period or which ground is close to "legal advice".
- **Further from the line?**
  - Selling blank, generic templates, such as the move-out pack or a template licence to a brokerage that fills the templates itself, looks more like publishing.
  - Typing exactly what a customer dictates looks more like a typing service.
  - Neither is confirmed.
- **Uncertain:**
  - whether the Federal and Dubai rules have any exception for template or typing services by non-lawyers
  - whether "to the public" in AR 52/2022 covers one-to-one WhatsApp orders
  - which DET activity, if any, covers template-filling without LAD approval
  - whether a payment provider (Ziina flags "legal service providers") would treat you as a legal service

## 5. Recommendation

**The cheapest legal setup to start taking payments, subject to the VERIFY items:**

1. **Before any licence, phone DET** (or use the Invest in Dubai portal) with three questions:
   - Can an expat resident employee get a professional e-Trader licence in 2026, and in what form must the employer NOC be?
   - What is the total cost? The sources say about AED 1,370 a year.
   - What is the exact activity code for preparing standard documents from templates or typing, and does it need LAD approval?
2. **Then see the lawyer** (section 5.1) before you spend the AED 1,370. If the lawyer says per-customer filling is legal consultancy, the e-Trader will not cure that.
3. **If both are clear, get the e-Trader licence.** About AED 1,370 a year is the lowest-cost Dubai option found. The alternatives start at about AED 5,750 a year (section 1.4).
4. **Payments:**
   - **Ziina Professional or Business** is the cheapest and quickest for WhatsApp links: 2.6% + AED 1 on UAE cards, no monthly fee found, payout in 1–2 days. It can start before the licence is verified, but describe the business accurately; "legal service providers" are on its restricted list.
   - **Stripe UAE** suits the AED 150 a month broker subscription once you hold a licence or permit: 2.9% + AED 1, T+5 payouts.
   - **Bank transfer** for brokerages: plain invoice, no VAT, below AED 375,000.
5. **Until then:** keep selling through platforms that pay you out, such as the Fiverr gig and the storefront in the playbook. Confirm with the lawyer whether that structure changes the analysis.

### 5.1 Three questions to take to a licensed UAE lawyer

1. Under Art. 5 of Federal Decree-Law 34/2022 ("drafting the contracts and relevant legal procedures") and Dubai Administrative Resolution 52/2022 ("Legal Services … drafting contracts … to the public"):
   - Is a non-lawyer filling Dubai tenancy notices and letters (rent-increase, non-renewal, deposit demand) with a customer's own facts, for a fee, a restricted legal service?
   - Does it change anything if I never choose the notice type, period or grounds for the customer?
2. Is selling or licensing blank templates with general guidance outside the restricted scope? This covers the move-out pack and a branded template pack that brokerages fill themselves. What disclaimers, wording or limits keep it outside?
3. Which licence or activity code covers what I may legally do? For example: an e-Trader professional activity such as typing or document services, a legal-consultancy route via the LAD, or working under a licensed firm. What penalty applies if this is misjudged, under Art. 97/98 and Executive Council Resolution 22/2011?

General information and templates only, not legal advice. For your specific case consult a licensed legal practitioner in the UAE.
